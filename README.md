# Elementbound

An original elemental-bending action game you can play free in your browser. It comes in two parts:

- **Elementbound (2D):** a hand-drawn Metroidvania. Explore five regions, learn air, water, earth and fire, and fight the bosses.
- **Elementbound 3D, Chapter 1: Kite Hill:** a third-person action chapter with weighty staff combat and air bending.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repo, then pick 2D or 3D.

## Controls
| | Controller | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2D** | Stick move · ✕ jump · □ strike · ○ dash · L1/L2/R1/R2 bending | Arrows move · Space jump · Z strike · X or Shift dash · A S D F bending · Q heal · ↑/E interact · Tab map · Esc pause | Left thumbstick · Jump, Strike, Dash and bending buttons on the right |
| **3D** | See the in-game Controls page (pause menu) | WASD move · mouse look · left-click strike (hold for heavy) · Space jump · Shift dodge · Q guard/parry · E interact · Tab/middle-click lock on | Left thumbstick · drag to look · Attack and action buttons on the right |

Both games show the right button prompts for whatever you used last.

## How it was made
Built with Claude (Anthropic): the code, the design and an automated playtest loop. All the art, animation, 3D models and music were generated with Higgsfield. The sound effects come from ElevenLabs.

The 3D game uses [three.js](https://threejs.org) (MIT licence; see `3d/vendor/three/LICENSE`).
